Deity information

Descriptions of each deity’s qualities, symbols, festivals, and stories were written for this project as general educational summaries, informed by widely available introductory sources on Hindu traditions, including the World History Encyclopedia and Wikipedia. They are not a complete or authoritative religious reference, and traditions vary by region and community.
