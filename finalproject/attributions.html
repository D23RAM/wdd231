Deity paintings

Images are traditional paintings, mostly Kalighat bazaar paintings from Kolkata (c. 1890) and Pahari/Basohli court paintings, from the Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access collection (CC0). Radha and Sita are from the World History Encyclopedia, and Kartikeya is from the Wellcome Collection. Images are used with credit and are only resized and converted to WebP for faster loading.

Deity information

Descriptions of each deity’s qualities, symbols, festivals, and stories were written for this project as general educational summaries, informed by widely available introductory sources on Hindu traditions, including the World History Encyclopedia and Wikipedia. They are not a complete or authoritative religious reference, and traditions vary by region and community.
