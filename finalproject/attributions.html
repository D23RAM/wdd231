Deity paintings

The images used in this project include traditional Kalighat bazaar paintings from Kolkata, dating to approximately 1890, and Pahari and Basohli court paintings. Most are from the Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access collection and are identified as CC0 or public domain. Radha and Sita images are sourced from the World History Encyclopedia, and Kartikeya is sourced from the Wellcome Collection. Images have been resized and converted to WebP for faster loading.

  • Parvati: “Parvati Placing a Wedding Garland on Shiva (recto), from a Kalighat album,” Kalighat artist, c. 1890. Cleveland Museum of Art, accession 2003.108.a. License: CC0 / Public Domain (Open Access). View original source.
  • Vishnu: “Vishnu on Ananta, the Endless Serpent,” Pahari school, Chamba, c. 1700. Cleveland Museum of Art, accession 2018.155. License: CC0 / Public Domain (Open Access). View original source.

Deity information

Descriptions of each deity's qualities, symbols, festivals, and stories were written for this project as general educational summaries. They were informed by introductory resources, including the World History Encyclopedia and Wikipedia. These summaries are not a complete or authoritative religious reference. Hindu traditions and interpretations vary across regions, communities, and texts.
