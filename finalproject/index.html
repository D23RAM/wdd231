What you'll find here

Hindu Deities Explorer is a student web-development project built to introduce visitors to major figures in Hindu tradition — the qualities they represent, the symbols associated with them, the festivals that honor them, and a mantra connected to each.

Search and filter

Look up a deity by name, or filter by category — from the Trimurti to beloved goddesses and devoted protectors.

Detailed profiles

Open any deity's profile for their story, associated qualities, symbols, festivals, and a mantra.

Save favorites

Mark the deities you'd like to revisit — your favorites are remembered on your device.
