Hindu Deities Explorer

Website Plan

1. Site Name

Hindu Deities Explorer

I chose the name Hindu Deities Explorer because it clearly explains what the website is about. The website is meant to let people explore different Hindu deities, their stories, symbols, qualities, and traditions in a simple and interactive way.

Possible domain: hindudeitiesexplorer.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Hindu Deities Explorer is to create an interactive educational website where people can learn about different Hindu deities and traditions. Visitors will be able to explore information about each deity, including their stories, qualities, symbols, festivals, and other important details. The goal is to make learning about Hinduism easier and more interesting, especially for people who may not know much about it.

3. Scenarios

These are some questions that someone visiting the website might have:

  • Who is Lord Ganesha and what do his different symbols represent?
  • What are some of the main stories associated with Lord Shiva?
  • Which Hindu festivals are connected to different deities?
  • What qualities or values are associated with each deity?

4. Color Scheme

I chose colors that are inspired by traditional Indian and Hindu celebrations. Saffron, maroon, gold, and warm ivory give the website a spiritual and traditional feeling while still keeping it easy to read.

5. Typography

I want the typography to have a traditional feeling while still being easy to read. I will use two fonts throughout the website.

Cormorant Garamond

Cormorant Garamond will be used for the main headings, deity names, and other important titles. Its classic appearance gives the website a more traditional feeling.

Lato

Lato will be used for body text, navigation, buttons, and other interface elements because it is simple and easy to read.

6. Wireframe

Small Viewport

The mobile version will use a simple vertical layout. The website name and navigation will be at the top, followed by an introduction and featured deity cards.
