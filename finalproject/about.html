About this project

Hindu Deities Explorer is a WDD 231 term project built to introduce visitors to major figures in Hindu tradition in a clear, respectful, and educational way. It is a personal learning project, not an official or exhaustive religious resource, and interpretations of these figures vary across regions, communities, and lineages.

  • Respectful presentationEach profile is written in plain, descriptive language and avoids treating these traditions as folklore or myth in a dismissive sense.
  • Accuracy over simplicityWhere traditions differ in their interpretation of a deity, the text avoids making a single claim sound absolute.
  • Built from scratchEvery page, style, and script on this site was written for this course assignment, without templates or frameworks.
