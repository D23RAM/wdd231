NP Membership

NP Membership is designed for nonprofit organizations and has no membership fee.

  • Business directory listing
  • Community networking opportunities
  • Access to selected chamber events

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership provides essential resources for businesses beginning to build their local network.

  • Business directory listing
  • Networking opportunities
  • Access to selected training sessions
  • Member event discounts

Silver Membership

Silver Membership provides increased visibility and access to additional business resources.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Additional training opportunities
  • Increased event discounts
  • Business promotion opportunities

Gold Membership

Gold Membership provides premium networking, promotional, and educational opportunities.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Premium networking opportunities
  • Priority promotional opportunities
  • Home page business spotlight eligibility
  • Additional event and training benefits
